Amazon Skills

What I can do for your Amazon store.

Product Research

Struggling to find a profitable product?

I'll find products with real demand and manageable competition, then check fees, landed cost, and margin so you can decide with confidence.

Supplier Sourcing

No time to find and talk to suppliers?

I'll shortlist and vet suppliers, compare quotes, arrange samples, and follow up so your order ships on spec and on time.

Product Listing

Is your listing ranking well?

Keyword-rich titles, clear bullet points, and engaging descriptions that help your products get found and get sales. I also fix suppressed listings.

Amazon Sponsored Ads

Are your ads driving nowhere?

I'll plan and launch Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns based on your goal, whether that's more sales, a product launch, or brand visibility.

PPC Management

Is your ACoS too high?

I'll review your search term reports, harvest winning keywords, add negatives to cut wasted spend, and adjust bids to hit your ACoS target.

Customer Service & Account Health

Want happy customers and a healthy account?

I'll answer buyer messages quickly, monitor and respond to feedback, handle Seller Support cases, and keep your account health in good standing.
